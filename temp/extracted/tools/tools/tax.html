📖使用说明

用途与适用场景

本个税计算器适用于中国境内工薪阶层按月估算个人所得税及五险一金扣除后的实发工资。无论您是刚入职的新人、准备跳槽的职场人，还是 HR 人员核算薪酬，都可以使用本工具快速了解税前税后差异。

计算器严格遵循中国现行个人所得税「累计预扣预缴」计算方法：以当年截至本月累计收入减去累计免税收入、累计减除费用（每月 5000 元）、累计专项扣除（五险一金个人部分）和累计专项附加扣除后的余额为应纳税所得额，按七级超额累进税率计算本月应预扣预缴税额。

使用步骤

  • 输入税前月薪：填写您的月度税前工资总额。
  • 输入专项附加扣除：如子女教育、继续教育、大病医疗、住房贷款利息、住房租金、赡养老人等专项附加扣除的月度合计金额。
  • 调整五险一金比例：根据个人实际缴纳比例修改养老保险、医疗保险、失业保险和住房公积金的费率。默认值仅为常见参考值，各地政策差异较大。
  • 点击「计算」：页面将展示五险一金扣除额、应纳税所得额、适用税率、应缴个税及税后实发工资。
  • 查看全年推演：本工具同时展示当年第 1-12 个月的逐月个税变化，帮助您理解累计预扣预缴的「前低后高」特征。

FAQ

Q1：为什么年初个税少、年末个税多？
累计预扣预缴法下，随着累计收入增加，可能跨越更高税率级距，导致后期适用税率上升。这不是扣税错误，而是税法规定的正常累进现象。次年 3-6 月可通过个税汇算清缴多退少补。
Q2：年终奖如何计算？
本工具目前仅计算月薪个税。年终奖可选择单独计税或并入综合所得计税，两种方式的税负差异较大，建议结合具体金额另行计算。
Q3：五险一金比例和我实际缴纳的不一样怎么办？
请直接在输入框中修改为您实际缴纳的比例。不同城市、不同企业的缴纳基数和比例差异很大，默认值仅为常见参考，不代表您的实际情况。
Q4：计算结果与工资条不一致？
可能原因包括：1）五险一金缴纳基数与月薪不同（设有上下限）；2）存在补充公积金、企业年金等额外扣除；3）存在免税收入或额外扣除项。请以单位 HR 或工资条为准。
Q5：专项附加扣除有哪些项目？
目前包括：子女教育（每孩每月 2000 元）、3 岁以下婴幼儿照护（每孩每月 2000 元）、继续教育（每月 400 元或当年 3600 元）、大病医疗（据实扣除）、住房贷款利息（每月 1000 元）、住房租金（每月 800-1500 元，按城市）、赡养老人（每月 3000 元）。

Purpose & Use Cases

This income tax calculator is designed for salaried employees in China to estimate monthly individual income tax and net pay after social insurance and housing fund deductions. Whether you are a new hire, considering a job change, or an HR professional verifying payroll, this tool helps you quickly understand the difference between gross and net salary.

The calculator strictly follows China's current "cumulative withholding" method: taxable income is calculated as cumulative income minus cumulative tax-exempt income, cumulative standard deduction (5,000 CNY/month), cumulative special deductions (personal social insurance contributions), and cumulative special additional deductions. The result is then taxed using the seven-bracket progressive rate table.

How to Use

  • Enter monthly gross salary: Input your total monthly pre-tax salary.
  • Enter special additional deductions: Include monthly totals for child education, continuing education, serious illness medical expenses, housing loan interest, housing rent, and elderly parent support.
  • Adjust social insurance rates: Modify pension, medical, unemployment, and housing fund rates according to your actual contributions. Default values are common references and vary significantly by city.
  • Click "Calculate": The page will display social insurance deductions, taxable income, applicable tax rate, tax payable, and net salary.
  • View annual projection: The tool also shows monthly tax changes from month 1 to 12, helping you understand the "lower early, higher later" pattern of cumulative withholding.

FAQ

Q1: Why is my tax lower at the beginning of the year and higher at the end?
Under cumulative withholding, as your cumulative income increases, you may cross into higher tax brackets, causing the applicable rate to rise later in the year. This is not an error but the normal progressive effect mandated by tax law. Annual reconciliation from March to June of the following year allows for refunds or additional payments.
Q2: How is year-end bonus taxed?
This tool currently calculates monthly salary tax only. Year-end bonuses can be taxed separately or combined with comprehensive income, and the two methods can produce significantly different tax liabilities. We recommend calculating separately based on the specific amount.
Q3: What if my social insurance rates differ from the defaults?
Please edit the input fields to match your actual contribution rates. Different cities and employers have significantly different contribution bases and rates. The default values are common references and do not represent your specific situation.
Q4: Why do the results differ from my payslip?
Possible reasons include: 1) Social insurance contribution base differs from gross salary (subject to local min/max limits); 2) Additional deductions such as supplementary housing fund or enterprise annuity; 3) Tax-exempt income or extra deductions. Please refer to your employer's HR department or payslip as the final authority.
Q5: What items are included in special additional deductions?
Currently included: child education (2,000 CNY per child per month), care for children under 3 (2,000 CNY per child per month), continuing education (400 CNY/month or 3,600 CNY/year), serious illness medical expenses (actual amount), housing loan interest (1,000 CNY/month), housing rent (800-1,500 CNY/month depending on city), and elderly parent support (3,000 CNY/month).
