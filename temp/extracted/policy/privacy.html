Privacy Policy

最后更新日期：2026年9月8日

一、基本信息

本站（009tg下海导航）是一个由个人独立维护的静态导航网站。我们高度重视访客的隐私保护，本政策旨在说明本站如何处理与保护您的个人信息。

二、数据收集声明

本站作为静态导航站，不主动收集、存储或处理访客的个人身份信息（PII）。

您在本站浏览时，我们不会要求您注册账号、填写表单或提供任何个人资料。本站不设置用户登录系统，也不维护用户数据库。

然而，与绝大多数网站一样，本站托管服务提供商（Cloudflare）可能会记录基础的访问日志，例如：

  • IP 地址（用于安全防护与访问分析）
  • 浏览器类型与操作系统信息
  • 访问时间与页面请求记录

上述日志由托管服务商根据其隐私政策进行管理，站长本人无法直接访问或利用这些数据进行用户画像。

三、Cookie 使用说明

3.1 本站自身 Cookie

本站为改善用户体验，可能使用极少量的浏览器本地存储（localStorage）来记录您的主题偏好（深色/浅色模式）和语言偏好（中文/英文）。这些信息仅保存在您的本地浏览器中，不会上传至任何服务器。

3.2 第三方广告 Cookie

本站使用 Google AdSense 等第三方广告服务来展示广告。Google 及其合作伙伴可能使用 Cookie 来收集信息，以便：

  • 展示与您兴趣相关的个性化广告
  • 衡量广告效果并优化广告投放
  • 防止欺诈性广告活动

Google 使用广告 Cookie 使 Google 及其合作伙伴能够根据您访问本站及其他网站的情况向您投放广告。

您可以通过以下方式管理或退出个性化广告：

  • 访问 Google 广告设置 关闭个性化广告
  • 访问 About Ads 选择退出基于兴趣的广告
  • 在浏览器设置中清除或阻止第三方 Cookie

请注意，即使您选择退出个性化广告，您仍可能看到非个性化广告。

四、第三方网站

本站包含大量指向第三方网站的外部链接。请注意：

  • 每个第三方网站均有其独立的隐私政策和使用条款
  • 本站不对任何第三方网站的内容、安全性或隐私实践负责
  • 点击外部链接离开本站后，您将不再受本隐私政策的约束
  • 建议您在向任何第三方网站提供个人信息前，仔细阅读其隐私政策

五、儿童隐私

本站不面向13岁以下儿童提供服务，亦不会有意收集13岁以下儿童的个人信息。如果您认为有儿童向我们提供了个人信息，请通过联系方式告知我们，我们将尽快删除相关信息。

六、政策更新

我们保留随时更新本隐私政策的权利。任何重大变更将在本页面显著位置公布，并更新"最后更新日期"。建议您定期查看本页面以了解最新政策。

如果您对本隐私政策有任何疑问，请通过联系方式与我们取得联系。

隐私政策

1. Overview

This website (009tg Navigation) is a static navigation site independently maintained by an individual. We highly value visitor privacy protection, and this policy explains how this site handles and protects your personal information.

2. Data Collection Statement

As a static navigation site, we do not actively collect, store, or process visitors' personally identifiable information (PII).

When you browse this site, we do not ask you to register an account, fill out forms, or provide any personal information. This site does not have a user login system and does not maintain a user database.

However, like most websites, our hosting service provider (Cloudflare) may record basic access logs, such as:

  • IP address (for security protection and access analysis)
  • Browser type and operating system information
  • Access time and page request records

The above logs are managed by the hosting provider according to its privacy policy. The webmaster cannot directly access or use this data for user profiling.

3. Cookie Usage

3.1 Our Own Cookies

To improve user experience, this site may use a minimal amount of browser local storage (localStorage) to record your theme preference (dark/light mode) and language preference (Chinese/English). This information is stored only in your local browser and is not uploaded to any server.

3.2 Third-Party Advertising Cookies

This site uses Google AdSense and other third-party advertising services to display ads. Google and its partners may use cookies to collect information in order to:

  • Display personalized ads based on your interests
  • Measure ad effectiveness and optimize ad delivery
  • Prevent fraudulent advertising activities

Google uses advertising cookies to enable Google and its partners to serve ads to you based on your visits to this site and/or other sites on the Internet.

You can manage or opt out of personalized advertising through the following methods:

  • Visit Google Ad Settings to turn off personalized ads
  • Visit About Ads to opt out of interest-based advertising
  • Clear or block third-party cookies in your browser settings

Please note that even if you opt out of personalized advertising, you may still see non-personalized ads.

4. Third-Party Websites

This site contains numerous external links to third-party websites. Please note:

  • Each third-party website has its own independent privacy policy and terms of use
  • This site is not responsible for the content, security, or privacy practices of any third-party website
  • After clicking an external link and leaving this site, you will no longer be bound by this privacy policy
  • We recommend that you carefully read the privacy policy of any third-party website before providing personal information

5. Children's Privacy

This site does not target services to children under 13 years of age and does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please inform us through our Contact page, and we will promptly delete the relevant information.

6. Policy Updates

We reserve the right to update this privacy policy at any time. Any significant changes will be posted in a prominent position on this page, and the "Last Updated" date will be revised. We recommend that you review this page regularly for the latest policy.

If you have any questions about this privacy policy, please contact us through our Contact page.
