📖使用说明

用途与适用场景

本房贷计算器适用于购房贷款、商业贷款、公积金贷款等场景的月供与总利息估算。无论您是准备购房的首次置业者，还是希望对比不同还款方式的已有贷款人，都可以使用本工具快速获得还款概览。

等额本息模式下，每月还款金额固定，适合收入稳定、希望规划固定支出的借款人；等额本金模式下，每月本金固定、利息逐月递减，前期还款压力较大但总利息更少，适合前期资金充裕的借款人。

使用步骤

  • 选择还款方式：在「等额本息」「等额本金」「复利计算」三个标签页之间切换。
  • 输入贷款金额：以万元为单位填写贷款总额。
  • 输入年利率：填写银行给出的贷款年利率（如 3.85%）。
  • 输入贷款年限：以年为单位填写贷款期限（常见为 10-30 年）。
  • 查看结果：页面自动计算并展示月供、还款总额、支付利息总额等关键数据。
  • 等额本金模式可展开查看前 12 期逐月还款明细。

FAQ

Q1：计算结果与银行实际扣款完全一致吗？
本工具按标准数学公式计算，结果与银行口径基本一致，但实际扣款可能因计息天数差异（大小月）、利率调整、提前还款等因素存在微小差异，请以银行账单为准。
Q2：等额本息和等额本金哪种更划算？
等额本金总利息更少，但前期月供较高；等额本息月供固定、前期压力小但总利息较多。选择哪种方式取决于您的收入结构和现金流规划。
Q3：LPR 利率调整后如何重新计算？
当 LPR 调整时，您可将新的执行利率填入「年利率」字段，即可重新计算后续月供。注意等额本息模式下，利率调整后银行通常会保持月供基本不变、调整还款期限。
Q4：复利计算模式适用于什么场景？
复利计算适用于投资增值、存款收益、理财回报等非贷款场景，帮助您估算本金在复利增长下的终值。
Q5：组合贷款（公积金+商业）怎么算？
建议分别计算公积金贷款部分和商业贷款部分，然后将两组结果相加，即可获得组合贷款的总月供和总利息。

Purpose & Use Cases

This mortgage calculator is designed for estimating monthly payments and total interest for home purchase loans, commercial loans, and provident fund loans. Whether you are a first-time homebuyer or an existing borrower comparing repayment methods, this tool provides a quick repayment overview.

Equal Principal+Interest (EPP) mode offers fixed monthly payments, suitable for borrowers with stable income who prefer predictable expenses. Equal Principal (EP) mode has fixed principal and decreasing interest, resulting in higher early payments but lower total interest, ideal for those with ample initial funds.

How to Use

  • Select repayment method: Switch between "Equal Principal+Interest", "Equal Principal", and "Compound Interest" tabs.
  • Enter loan amount: Input the total loan amount in ten-thousands.
  • Enter annual interest rate: Fill in the annual rate provided by your bank (e.g. 3.85%).
  • Enter loan term: Input the loan duration in years (commonly 10-30 years).
  • View results: The page automatically calculates and displays monthly payment, total repayment, and total interest.
  • Equal Principal mode can expand to show the first 12 months of repayment details.

FAQ

Q1: Do the results exactly match my bank statement?
This tool uses standard mathematical formulas and should align closely with bank calculations. However, actual deductions may vary slightly due to day-count differences, rate adjustments, or early repayments. Always refer to your bank statement as the final authority.
Q2: Which is better, EPP or EP?
EP has lower total interest but higher initial payments. EPP has fixed payments with lower initial burden but higher total interest. The choice depends on your income structure and cash flow planning.
Q3: How do I recalculate after an LPR rate adjustment?
Enter the new effective rate in the "Annual Rate" field to recalculate. Note that for EPP loans, banks typically keep the monthly payment roughly unchanged and adjust the remaining term instead.
Q4: What is the compound interest mode for?
Compound interest mode is for investment growth, deposit returns, and wealth management scenarios. It helps estimate the future value of principal under compound growth.
Q5: How do I calculate a combined loan?
Calculate the provident fund portion and the commercial loan portion separately, then add the two results together for the combined total monthly payment and total interest.
