About Us

Introduction

009tg Navigation was founded in 2017, originally born from the pain point of cross-border e-commerce practitioners struggling to manage overflowing browser bookmarks. After years of continuous operation and iteration, it has evolved into a comprehensive web directory focused on curating quality internet tools and resources, dedicated to helping users quickly discover and access the most practical websites, software, and online tools available.

Our mission is making quality tools accessible to everyone. Every listed website undergoes rigorous screening to ensure safety, reliability, and practical efficiency. Whether you are an entrepreneur, developer, marketer, or an everyday internet user, you will find tools here to boost productivity and expand your business horizons.

Categories Covered

Our curated tools and resources cover the following core areas:

  • AI Tools: AI writing, image generation, video creation, coding assistance, AI agents
  • Cross-border E-commerce: platforms, product research, ERP systems, logistics & warehousing
  • Overseas Marketing: ad networks, SEO optimization, traffic exchange, email marketing
  • Social Media: content creation, account management, data analytics, follower growth
  • Global Network: VPS, domain registration, CDN acceleration, DNS services
  • SMS Verification: virtual numbers, SMS reception, privacy protection
  • Cryptocurrency: exchanges, wallets, market analysis
  • Global Payments: payment platforms, collection tools, cross-border transfers
  • Anti-detect Browsers: fingerprint browsers, multi-account management

Additionally covering advertising tools, content creation, tech communities, traffic tools, cross-border services, and more — totaling 19 primary categories and 86 subcategories.

Key Features

  • Category Detail Pages: Each category has a dedicated detail page showing all tools with pagination support
  • Bilingual Support: Full Chinese and English language versions serving global users
  • Local Favicon Cache: All website icons cached locally for faster loading and stability
  • Redirect Protection: All external links go through a redirect page for user privacy and security
  • Responsive Design: Perfectly adapts to desktop, tablet, and mobile screen sizes
  • Static Hosting: Powered by Cloudflare Pages global CDN for fast access and high reliability

Who Is This For?

This directory is especially useful for:

  • Cross-border E-commerce Sellers: Quickly find tools for product research, operations, logistics, and payments
  • Overseas Marketers: Discover efficient tools for advertising, social media, and SEO optimization
  • AI Tool Enthusiasts: Experience the latest AI writing, design, coding, and agent tools in one place
  • Independent Developers & Entrepreneurs: Find practical tools to boost development efficiency and reduce operational costs
  • Everyday Internet Users: Discover quality online tools for daily work, learning, and life
